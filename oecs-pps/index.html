Agentic Rx Inc. supplies medicines, medical supplies and dialysis products from a Pharmacy Council-approved warehouse in Christ Church, Barbados, and builds the digital systems that health regulators and procurement services use. This console shows how we would manage OECS-PPS stock and documentation: every lot tracked first-expiry-first-out, cold-chain readings checked automatically, any lot traceable to every customer in minutes, and every manufacturer document (GMP, WHO-format CPP, authorisation letters) tracked to expiry.

What Agentic Rx offers OECS-PPS

  • Supply: OTC medicines (GoodSense/Perrigo via Pinnacle Rx; Bayer OTC via Bioquímica Panamericana), prescription and wound-care products (Intercontinental Pharma), haemodialysis and laboratory supplies (Nipro), heparin (Gland Pharma).
  • Good distribution practice: SOP ARX-GDP-001 (receipt, storage, FEFO, recall, disposal) and ARX-QA-004 (complaints), run through the inventory system shown here.
  • Documentation on request: CoA, GMP, WHO-format CPP and licences per product, tracked and refreshed before expiry.
  • Technology and regulatory support: the same team built the Barbados Medical Products Authority consultation portal and the RAGNA 2.0 Task Force member platform (see Regulatory support).

Cold chain: 2–8 °C store, last 7 days

Data loggers record the cold store every 15 minutes. Readings outside 2–8 °C for more than 30 minutes open an excursion record automatically; the pharmacist assesses the affected lots and releases or quarantines them, and the decision is kept with the lot history.

Regulatory support: application management for regulators and procurement services

Alongside supply, Agentic Rx builds the systems regulators use to consult, register and coordinate. We propose to support OECS-PPS and national regulators with a product registration and supplier prequalification workflow built on the same approach as our work below.

Barbados Medical Products Authority: bmpra.com

Designed, built and host the national public consultation portal for the Medical Products Act, 2026: a section-by-section guide to the Act, a page-by-page viewer for the draft Medical Products and Clinical Trial Regulations, medicines lists and document library, WHO benchmarking page, an AI assistant that answers with references, and a public comment tool that routes submissions to the consultation inbox.

Client: Barbados Medical Products Authority with the Barbados Drug Service, Ministry of Health and Wellness · www.bmpra.com

RAGNA 2.0 Task Force: member portal and secure collaboration

Designed and built the public website and a role-based member portal for the Regulatory Agencies Global Network against AMR, coordinated with Läkemedelsverket (Swedish Medical Products Agency), and deployed a self-hosted collaboration platform: document management with in-browser editing, video meetings and chat, calendars, task boards, Secretariat/Member/Observer roles, two-factor sign-in and an on-server AI assistant.
