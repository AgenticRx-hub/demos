What the locator does

  • Sees assigned tasks sorted by due time, with navigation to the dig site.
  • Opens the map with City layers at the excavation area.
  • Records marks: facility, paint colour (APWA code), flags, offsets, notes.
  • Takes geotagged photos and a sketch; signs off.
  • Posts the positive response; works offline and syncs when back in coverage.

Routing rules and administration

City administrators set routing by facility type and service area, due-time rules, escalation and users, without the vendor.

Escalation

  • Emergency ticket: SMS and email to on-call locator and supervisor within 1 minute.
  • 4 hours before due: reminder to assignee.
  • At due time with no response: supervisor alert; ticket flagged overdue.

Security and records

  • US-hosted, encrypted at rest and in transit; daily backups.
  • Single sign-on with City accounts; role-based access; full audit log.
  • Records retained per Chapter 119 F.S. and the City's retention schedule; City owns and can export all data.
