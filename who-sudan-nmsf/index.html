Key experts and level of effort

Data sources and assumptions

Data is used in this order: the client's own data, then real-world public data, then sample data, then modelled estimates. Every figure in this tool is labelled.

From the RFP RFP

  • Sixteen assessment domains (5.3.2 a to p), fifteen specific objectives (5.3.1) and the six-phase methodology (5.4.1).
  • Four-level capacity scale and risk register (5.4.2); one-page dashboard columns (5.4.3); priority bands 0–3, 3–6, 6–12 and 12+ months (5.4.4).
  • Minimum of six state-level sites across five zones: Central, Eastern, Northern, Kordofan and Darfur (5.4.1 Phase 3).
  • Deliverables and weeks (5.4.5, 5.6), interim briefing by Week 5, payment schedule 15/25/30/30% with no advance (5.8).
  • Four key experts and evaluation weights (Section 4): 200 / 450 / 350 points, 70% pass mark, 70:30 technical to financial.

Real-world context Public

  • Sudan has 18 states; the state names and zones used here are real.
  • Conflict since April 2023 has disrupted Khartoum-based operations; national supply functions have relied on Port Sudan (Red Sea) as the main entry and hub. Treated here as the central hub; to be confirmed at inception.
  • Access to parts of Darfur and Kordofan is restricted, which is why the RFP allows alternative sites and verification methods.
  • SHEIP is financed by the African Development Bank and supported by WHO (ToR 5.1.1).

Sample data Sample

  • Pallet capacities, current use and damaged share per warehouse; cold storage litres; fleet size.
  • Base domain ratings, evidence and gaps on the dashboard; risk likelihood and impact; action costs.
  • Transaction step pass rates (receipt to proof of delivery).
  • All replaced with NMSF evidence collected in Weeks 1–7 (Deliverable 2).

Modelled assumptions Model

  • Stored pallets from SHEIP = monthly volume × dwell months; peak = in-use × peak multiplier.
  • Warehousing rating: 3 if peak utilisation under 80%, 2 if 80–95%, 1 above 95%.
  • Lead time = base days × (1 + closure factor × (100 − access)/100), with closure factor 2.5 in Darfur, 1.8 in Kordofan, 0.6 elsewhere.
  • Truck carries 16 pallets and makes 3 round trips a month; base network demand 1,400 pallets a month.
  • Generator covers up to 14 hours a day when 3 or more days of fuel are in stock, otherwise 4 hours per day of fuel; excursions ≈ 0.9 per uncovered hour per month per site, cut by 85% with solar direct-drive.
  • Margin of error = 1.96 × √(p(1−p)/n). End-to-end pass rate = product of step pass rates.
  • Rehabilitation at USD 180 per pallet position restored. Spend accrues evenly across active weeks.
