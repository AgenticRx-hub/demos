Products, manufacturers and revision dates here are illustrative; at start-up they are replaced by BLM's own inventory, imported from the SQLite3 file BLM provides (SOW 4.1).

Search the SDS library

Search by product, manufacturer, CAS number or hazard; filter by district and facility (SOW 3.1.3, 3.1.17). Select a row to view, print or add the SDS to a facility binder.

Facility electronic binders and workbooks

Each of the 74 facilities has its own binder (SOW 3.1.5, 3.1.6). Download or print the workbook, or post the facility QR code so staff on site can open the binder from a phone (3.1.7, 3.1.8, 3.1.9, 3.1.23).

Facility QR code

Scanning opens this facility's binder on any phone or tablet. The binder is also cached for offline use in the field.

Manufacturer updates, alerts and archive

The service checks every SDS against manufacturer revisions and GHS changes, alerts administrators, and keeps superseded versions in an archive for the retention period (SOW 3.1.10, 3.1.11, 3.1.16, 3.1.21).

Administrators and users

BLM manages its own users without asking the vendor (3.1.13): one State-level Primary Account Administrator and at least twelve support administrators who can be rotated, all with edit access to every facility (3.1.14, 3.1.15).
