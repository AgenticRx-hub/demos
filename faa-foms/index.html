FOMS Mobile on iPad: itinerary, risk assessment, offline daily flight log

Crew members download an assigned itinerary, complete the risk assessment before flight release, and fill the daily flight log (DFL) offline in flight. Changes queue on the device and sync when a connection returns. Toggle Offline to see the queue.

FOMS Mobile · Capt. R. Alvarez (PIC) · Synced

Built in Swift/SwiftUI with on-device storage, background sync over REST, APN messaging and enterprise SSO with multi-factor authentication (SOW Appendix A, Development 1–10).

Mission Planner: aircraft, crew and CAMP maintenance windows

Scheduling of aircraft, crew and missions with Not-To-Exceed (NTE) maintenance times pulled from the new CAMP maintenance system (SOW Web Development 1). Move the slider: missions that would push an aircraft past its NTE are flagged before they are assigned.

Flight Inspection Scheduling Tool (FIST): inspection tasks on the map

A geographical view of open inspection tasks (ILS, VOR, RNAV procedures, commissioning) and a proposed itinerary. Pick a base and the itinerary re-plans to the nearest due tasks.

FITS & FIRPS: inspection objects, tasks, costs and reports

Flight Inspection Tracking System: object and task status plus reimbursable costs. Click a task to see its Flight Inspection Report Processing (FIRPS) workflow.

Fuel Billing & Reconciliation (FBR)

Fuel tickets captured on the iPad are matched automatically to vendor invoices and card statements. Set the tolerance to see how many tickets clear without manual review.
