Auditor review queue

How a flag is made

  1. Claim and encounter data arrive from VistA through FHIR or HL7 v2 interfaces.
  2. Rules run: code-pair edits, units, ICD-10 to CPT consistency, documentation completeness, grouping and billing eligibility.
  3. Natural-language extraction finds evidence in the note and links each finding to a page and passage.
  4. The auditor accepts or rejects. Nothing changes a claim without a human decision.
  5. Every decision is logged and feeds accuracy reporting and coder education.

AI coding assistant

Guardrails

  • Answers come only from the encounter record, the note and the rule library. Every claim of fact carries a citation to the note and page.
  • If the note does not support an answer, the assistant says so and suggests a provider query instead of guessing.
  • The assistant suggests; the certified coder or auditor decides. It cannot change a claim.
  • Prompts and answers are logged with the rule and model version for audit.
  • In this demo the assistant is rule-based and runs in your browser. No data leaves the page.
