Data & assumptions

From the RFP RFP

  • Five states: Anambra, Ebonyi, Gombe, Kwara and Lagos.
  • Global Fund RSSH pays State health insurance premiums for PLHIV and TB clients for at least two years.
  • More than 35,000 enrolled; 71% female.
  • ToR tasks 4.1 to 4.9: workflow mapping, SOPs, ICT assessment and digitalisation roadmap, community engagement, capacity building, supportive supervision, facility mapping and accreditation, strategic plans, enrolment dashboard.
  • Eight weeks; deliverables as listed in the workplan tab.

Sample data Sample

  • State split of the 35,000 enrolled and the state targets (51,000 in total).
  • PLHIV and TB shares per state.
  • Funnel step rates and causes of loss.
  • Digital maturity scores (1 to 5) across six dimensions.
  • Facility counts and accredited shares.
  • Premium of ₦15,000 per enrollee per year.

Model rules Model

  • Monthly enrolments per state = officers × 14 × a state-size factor × (1 + 0.6 × digital share) + 45% of community referrals, capped by validation capacity, which falls as turnaround rises.
  • Validation backlog = monthly enrolments × turnaround ÷ 30.
  • Funnel: harmonised SOPs cut the loss at each step by up to 60%; digital capture cuts registration and validation losses by up to 50%; NIN coverage drives validation success.
  • Accreditation: visits × weeks × first-visit pass rate, plus re-visits for those that failed.
  • Financing: Global Fund pays years 1–2; from year 3, state funds cover the share set, growing yearly; the rest is the gap.

What changes at inception

  • Each SSHIA's enrolment register, validation logs and premium records replace the sample data.
  • Facility lists come from NHIA, NPHCDA and the state HIV and TB programmes.
  • The dashboard is handed over to the SSHIAs with the data dictionary and training (ToR 4.9).
