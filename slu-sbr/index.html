How an upgraded SBR could look for the Central Statistics Office: one register of enterprises and establishments fed by administrative sources, consistent ISIC coding, quality checks, survey frames and business demography, built on the CSO's existing SQL relational database.

Coverage by source (sample)

Six-month delivery path

Situational analysis using the UN Maturity Model for Statistical Business Registers. Each dimension gets a current level, a target level and the actions that close the gap. Together these form the SBR Improvement Plan.

Register browser: enterprises, legal units and establishments with status, ISIC code, size class and the administrative sources that confirm them.

Administrative data integration: each custodian's file is loaded, standardised and matched to the register (exact ID match first, then probabilistic name and address matching), and unmatched records go to a review queue.

Match review queue (sample)

Classification consistency and the ISIC Rev.4 → Rev.5 migration plan: correspondence, one-to-many splits flagged for review, and the share of units affected.

Quality assurance against the UN National Quality Assurance Framework: automated checks on every load, with results the CSO can track over time.

Survey sampling frames and business demography: extract a frame by sector and size, and see births, deaths and survival from the register.

Frame extract

Frames are versioned and frozen, so every survey can be traced back to the exact register snapshot it used.
