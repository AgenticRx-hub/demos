Base-year progress (sample)

How a count day runs

  • 07:30 Entrance meeting with the VA Pharmacy representative; confirm areas and VA-furnished data (prime-vendor price file, direct-purchase costs, automation data, CS data).
  • 08:00 Count starts. Each section is tagged with a start and end marker and assigned to one specialist.
  • During Section printouts reviewed with VA staff; recounts done on the spot.
  • Close Facility walkthrough, markers removed, closeout sheet signed.
  • Day 1–5 QC checks, pricing validation, CS and automation data merged.
  • ≤ Day 7 Final report delivered (XLSX + PDF summary).

Proposed base-year schedule. Two crews work in parallel on weekends so all 12 inventories finish by 24 January 2027, leaving the weekend of 30–31 January as a reserve. That meets both the 28 February deadline and the stricter 31 January date in SOW §4. Reports are due 7 calendar days after each count.

Section control (SOW §13): every section has a start and end marker, one responsible specialist and a status, so no shelf is counted twice or missed when several specialists work at once. VA staff can see at a glance what is finished.

Partial-container rule (SOW §12)

  • Opened bottles: estimated to 0.1 of a bottle.
  • High-cost items (package price ≥ $500, sample threshold): counted in whole units.
  • Compounding stock: excluded from 0.1 estimation per SOW.
  • The method is documented in the report header and applied the same way at every site.

Recorded lines (sample)

Automated dispensing and packaging equipment (SOW §9–10). VA Pharmacy provides each system's on-hand export; we map it to NDC and McKesson numbers and merge it into the report without recounting.

Controlled substances (SOW §11)

Counted only by authorised VA Pharmacy personnel. Our crew does not count, handle, move or reconcile CS. The VA-provided CS count file is imported read-only, kept in its own report section, and included in the facility total.

Discrepancies are logged, never silently fixed. Contractor-caused errors are corrected at no cost; anything that looks wrong in VA-furnished data is documented and clarified with VA Pharmacy before any change (SOW §14).

Automatic quality-control checks run before any report leaves our hands (SOW §21–22).

Final inventory report, one per pharmacy, with every SOW §20 field. Each item is a separate record, numbers stay numeric, NDCs keep leading zeros, and the extended cost always equals quantity × unit price.
